How to install

  1. Download and unpack the Interlude client.
  2. Download the L2Disciple system and copy its files into the client's system folder, replacing the existing files.
  3. Start the game with system\l2.exe and log in.

Rules

Zero tolerance. Read this once, there won't be a second explanation.

  1. Cheating = ban. Bots, third-party programs, modified clients, exploiting bugs: you get banned, and every character that played from your IP address is deleted. No warnings, no second chances.
  2. Max 2 clients per IP. At most 2 game clients online at the same time from one internet connection; the server refuses a third.
  3. The admin decides. Only the admin. Rates, features and everything else about this server. Requests and suggestions are not accepted. Don't like something? Pick a different server — you're free to leave.
  4. No donations. None accepted, nothing for sale, ever.
  5. No real-money trading. Buying or selling adena, items, characters or accounts for real money is treated like cheating (rule 1).
  6. No scamming. Same zero tolerance.
  7. Don't be a jerk. Not to other players, not to the staff.
  8. Don't bother the staff. Found a bug? Report it once, in #bug-reports on our Discord. Anything else: see rule 3.

Features

Interlude as it was on retail: skills, quests, NPC stats, drops, spoil and items were checked against lineage2wiki. Olympiad, Seven Signs, castle sieges, the manor, grand bosses, the Four Sepulchers, Monster Race and fishing are all in. Some things may still be broken — report them on Discord. On top of that:

Commands (type them in chat)

  • .commands — lists every command below.
  • .nobuff — turns buffs from other players on or off.
  • .expoff / .expon — stop or resume gaining experience on your character (you still earn SP; stays off until you type .expon).
  • .deposit / .withdraw — bank: 500,000,000 adena ↔ 1 Gold Bar.
  • .offline — keep your private store (sell, buy or craft) open while you are offline, in towns, for up to 7 days.

Community Board (Alt+B)

  • Server info, the command list, news and rules.

NPCs

  • Delevel Manager — lowers your level by 1 for 1,000,000 adena.
  • Matild — brews a Mana Potion from 10 Crystal: D-Grade, 2 Coarse Bone Powder and 1 Greater Healing Potion (60% chance).

Known issues

  • The server has no geodata: monsters can see and attack through walls, and there is no fall damage (Acrobat and Iron Body have no effect).
  • Many fixes are new and still being tested. Found a bug? Report it once on Discord (rule 8).

Not bugs (same as retail Interlude)

  • Some older items have no recipe or part source and can't be crafted (for example Shining Bow).
  • Dual swords need Dualsword Craft Stamps, which come from the manor — so only once a clan owns a castle.
